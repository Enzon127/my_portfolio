Project Background

For today’s generation of pet owners, dogs are treasured family members. Yet many spend long hours alone while their people work. Blōg began with a simple question: how can owners ease the worry of leaving their pets behind?

Research

INTERVIEWED

Health changes feel urgent

Sudden changes in health are the most worrisome. Owners want faster and more reliable support for deciding what to do next.

02

Location cannot help diagnosis

Existing tools can show where a pet is or how much it moves but silent issues and abnormal trends remain hard to understand.

03

From data to immediate dicisions

Real-time alerts and trend-based suggestions are preferred over only sharing raw data with vets.

04

Wearability matters

Comfort, pet safety, and bite-resistant durability are considered essential.

05

Monitoring should be holistic

Owners want more than activity: temperature, breathing, heart rate, eating, sleep, and abnormal movement all matter.

06

Practicality drives adoption

Battery life and price strongly affect adoption, with all-day to one-week use per charge setting the expectation.

Problem Definition

The gap between seeing and knowing.

Due to the absence of an integrated home monitoring system, health risks and emergencies often go unnoticed during extended unsupervised periods at home, resulting in delayed intervention and owners remaining in a state of persistent anxiety.

Pain Points

01 / Visibility

Invisible change

A location pin or video feed cannot interpret subtle symptoms when nobody is there to notice them.

02 / Reliability

Wearable worry

Owners need a comfortable, durable form that survives daily use without becoming another battery concern.

03 / Action

Data without direction

Generic scores and disconnected metrics do not tell owners what changed, how serious it is, or what to do next.

Our Solution

A connected care system for the home.

Blōg combines wearable sensing, edge intelligence, and a mobile application to make alerts and suggestions when you are away.

The system

Three layers, one continuous signal.

≈ 60 ms sensor to screen

Wearable sensing node

  • Clips onto existing collars.
  • Captures vital and behavioural signals.

Edge computing camera

  • Adds visual understanding.
  • App-controlled pitch and yaw.
  • Processes signals locally.

Mobile web application

  • Connects wearable and camera.
  • Provides trends for user reference.
  • Pushes emergency alerts.
